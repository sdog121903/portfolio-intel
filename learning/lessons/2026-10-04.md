# Lesson of the day, 2026-10-04

**Why won't your weekend orders fill at Friday's price?**

Your sheet lists 16 orders dated Monday 5 October, each with an "entry price" that is really
Friday's closing price. US stock markets are closed at weekends, so nothing trades until Monday
at 9:30 a.m. New York time (15:30 in Madrid).

A **market order** says "fill my order right away, at whatever the price is." When the market opens, your order
is filled at the first available price, which can be higher or lower than Friday's close,
because news over the weekend changes what buyers and sellers will pay.

A **limit order** says "buy only at this price or better." It protects you from a surprise
price, but it may never fill if the stock runs away from your limit.

*Worked example:* your sheet assumes Lumentum (LITE) at $1,085.42, so 0.018 shares cost
$19.54. If LITE opens 3% higher on Monday, at about $1,117.98, the same 0.018 shares cost
about $20.12: 58 cents more, before the market has done anything "to" you. That is why the
report shows zero profit or loss today: you do not own these shares yet.
