# Your learning folder

This is your growing textbook. The daily routine adds to it every morning.

- `lessons/` - every "Lesson of the day", one file per day. Reread them in order.
- `concepts.json` - every term you have been taught, when, and how well you know it (new,
  learning, known). The reports use it to decide how much to explain. You can edit it: mark a
  term "known" to stop seeing its definition, or "new" to get the full explanation again.
- `curriculum.md` - the path from beginner to expert. Ticked topics have been taught.
