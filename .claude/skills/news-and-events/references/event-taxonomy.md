# Event taxonomy

| Materiality | Event types | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Earnings results; guidance change; merger or acquisition; major contract won or lost; big customer change; CEO/CFO departure; restatement or auditor change; share sale (dilution) or large buyback; regulatory action, major lawsuit, export restriction; product recall or major outage; delisting notice | Changes expected profits or trust in the numbers |
| **Medium** | Analyst rating or target change with new information; new product launch or shipment start; partnership; investor-day targets; insider open-market purchases, or unplanned sales clusters; large holder (13D) stake; peer or customer results that change demand expectations; index inclusion | May change expectations; needs confirmation and context |
| **Low** | Conference appearances repeating known messages; minor partnerships; awards; routine insider transactions (tax withholding, planned 10b5-1 sales); generic market commentary; "stocks to watch" lists; price-prediction articles | Rarely changes anything; mention only if it explains a move |

Direction rule of thumb: ask "does this make future sales or profits more likely, less likely,
or no different, and does it change how sure investors can be about them?"
