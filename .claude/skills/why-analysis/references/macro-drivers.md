# Macro and policy transmission chains

How a big-picture event reaches the holdings. Use the chain, then say which holdings are most
exposed and why. Always cite the event itself.

| Event | Chain to the companies | Most exposed here |
|---|---|---|
| **Interest rates rise** (or are expected to) | Future profits are worth less today -> investors pay less for companies whose profits are far in the future -> fast-growing, highly valued stocks fall more | All; most for the most highly valued |
| **Big cloud companies raise or cut capital spending (capex)** | Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta and others buy chips, optics, cables and data-center buildings -> their budgets are these companies' revenue | Holdings tagged "AI data centers" in `config/portfolio.toml` `[themes]` (today CRDO, LITE, STRL, TSM, AMD, BE) directly |
| **Chip export controls tighten** | Rules limit selling advanced chips to some countries -> fewer possible customers -> lower expected sales | Chip makers (AMD, TSM) most; their suppliers (CRDO, LITE) indirectly |
| **Tariffs on imported components** | Higher input costs or retaliation -> lower margins or demand | Hardware makers with Asian supply chains |
| **A major cyberattack in the news** | Companies see the cost of weak security -> security budgets grow or shift vendors | CRWD, PANW (and their competitors) |
| **A security product failure** | Customers reconsider vendors -> renewals and new deals at risk | CRWD, PANW |
| **Construction costs or labor shortages** | Margins on fixed-price projects shrink | STRL |
| **"AI bubble" worries / a peer's disappointing results** | Investors fear AI spending peaks -> the whole AI supply chain sells off together, often regardless of each company's own news | The "AI data centers" holdings together (check correlation) |
| **Drug-pricing or vaccine-policy news** | Government price rules or vaccine recommendations change -> expected sales of medicines change | MRNA; A indirectly (drug makers buy its lab equipment) |
| **Energy and power-grid constraints** | Data centers cannot get grid power fast enough -> demand for on-site power and grid equipment | BE; the AI data-center holdings indirectly |
| **Recession fears** | Companies delay big purchases -> orders slow | All; infrastructure and hardware first |

When explaining a sector-wide sell-off, show it with the attribution numbers ("the industry part
explains most of the move") and the portfolio correlation ("your AI holdings tend to move together").
