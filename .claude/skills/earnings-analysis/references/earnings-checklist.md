# Earnings review checklist

Fill every line with a number and a source, or "not disclosed".

| # | Question | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue vs consensus (and vs last year) | Earnings release; consensus from tier-2 coverage |
| 2 | EPS (GAAP and non-GAAP) vs consensus | Release; reconciliation table |
| 3 | Next-quarter and full-year guidance vs consensus and vs prior guidance | Release, call transcript |
| 4 | Key industry metric(s) and trend (sector-kpis.md) | Release, investor presentation |
| 5 | Gross and operating margin vs last year: why did they move? | Release; 10-Q MD&A |
| 6 | Operating cash flow and free cash flow vs net income | Cash flow statement |
| 7 | Share count change (dilution or buybacks) | 10-Q; diluted share count |
| 8 | Customer concentration or big-customer comments | 10-Q notes; call Q&A |
| 9 | One-offs: restructuring, tax benefits, gains on investments | Reconciliation; notes |
| 10 | Management's stated reasons for any change | Call transcript prepared remarks and Q&A |
| 11 | Stock reaction: day move, and the market/industry/company split | Metrics and attribution files |
| 12 | Analyst reactions: rating and target changes, with reasons | Tier 2/3, labelled as opinion |

**Quality-of-beat flags** (each needs a sentence if present): revenue beat but guidance below
consensus; EPS beat driven by tax or share count rather than operations; margins up only because
spending was postponed; receivables or inventory growing much faster than sales; cash flow far
below profit; big gap between GAAP and adjusted profit that is widening.

**One-sentence summary template:** "<Company> sold <more/less> than expected
(<revenue> vs <consensus>), but the stock <rose/fell> because <guidance / key metric / quality issue>."
