# Thesis status rubric

| Status | Evidence test | Typical examples | Report wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intact** | No tier-1/2 evidence against any "must stay true" pillar | Results in line or better; guidance kept or raised; normal market-driven moves | "Intact: nothing today contradicts why you own it." |
| **Watch** | One pillar weaker, cause plausibly temporary or unconfirmed; or a near-miss on a rule | Guidance trimmed for a stated timing reason; one big customer pausing; a short-seller report with unverified claims; price near his stop | "Watch: <pillar> looks weaker because <cause>; the test is <next event>." |
| **Challenged** | A pillar contradicted by tier-1/2 evidence, not yet an invalidation trigger | Growth slowing well below the thesis assumption for a second quarter; margin falling on competition; loss of a major customer confirmed | "Challenged: <pillar> is no longer supported because <evidence>." |
| **Broken** | An invalidation trigger in theses/<TICKER>.md clearly happened | Restatement; the core product lost its main customer; the business model changed | "Broken: <trigger> happened on <date> [source]." |

Rules:
- Move one step at a time unless a single event clearly justifies a bigger jump; explain any jump.
- Price alone never breaks a thesis; it can trigger his price rules, which are reported separately.
- Recovery works the same way: Watch -> Intact needs evidence that the weak pillar recovered.
