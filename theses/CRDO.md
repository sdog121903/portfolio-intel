## What the company does (plain English)
Credo makes the parts that let AI chips talk to each other at very high speed: active electrical
cables (copper cables with signal-boosting chips), chips for optical modules, and connectivity
chip designs. Its customers are mostly large cloud companies and the firms that build their hardware.

## How it makes money
Product sales (cables and chips) plus some licensing; the deep dive will add the split, with sources.

## Metrics that matter
- Revenue growth and guidance
- Gross margin
- Customer concentration (how much comes from the largest one or two customers)
- Adoption of its cables and chips in the newest data-center designs

## What must stay true (proposed starting points: edit or delete)

## Invalidation triggers (proposed starting points: edit or delete)
