## Why I own it (approved by Santi, 2026-10-05)

**In one sentence:** I hold FormFactor because every AI chip and AI memory chip must be tested on the wafer, and FormFactor makes the probe cards that do it, which customers keep re-buying.

Before chips are cut from a wafer, each one is tested with a probe card, a custom board with thousands of tiny needles. AI chips and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) are bigger and more complex, so they need more and harder testing, and a new chip design needs a new card while old cards wear out, so sales repeat (research file, product description). That shows in the numbers: revenue reached a record $258.2m (+31.9%) last quarter, driven by HBM [FormFactor Q2 2026 results, 2026-07-29], and memory maker Micron's latest sales beat expectations [Micron 8-K, 2026-09-30]. Deutsche Bank says it is now a second approved supplier of test cards for NVIDIA graphics chips (tier 3, unconfirmed), which could open a large new customer. It is a way to own the AI build-out through a tool every chipmaker needs.

**What I accept (the risks):** about a third of last quarter's margin gain was one-off, SK hynix alone is 24.3% of revenue, and after a 54% rise in a month it trades above the average analyst target ($138.63, tier 3).

## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Before chips are cut from a wafer, each one is tested by touching it with thousands of tiny needles on a "probe card", and FormFactor makes those cards. Each card is custom-made for one chip design and wears out, so chipmakers keep buying new ones, especially for the high-bandwidth memory (HBM) used in AI. Revenue was a record $258.2 million last quarter (+31.9%), and one customer, SK hynix, was 24.3% of it [FormFactor Q2 2026 results, 2026-07-29; research file, 10-Q].

## How it makes money
- Probe cards (custom test cards) for memory, including high-bandwidth memory, and for GPU and custom AI chips; buyers are memory makers and chip designers (research file).
- The split by product and segment is not in our files; the deep dive will add it.
- One customer, SK hynix, was 24.3% of revenue (research file, from the 10-Q).

   - *Counts if:* Q3 revenue below $260m (the bottom of the range), or Q4 guidance below Q3's actual revenue.

   - *Where and when to check:* Q3 results, about 2026-10-28.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
2. **Memory makers (especially SK hynix, 24.3% of revenue) cut spending.** *(Thesis 1)*

   - *Counts if:* SK hynix, Samsung or Micron cuts its spending plan or reports falling HBM output.
   - *Early warning:* Talk of HBM oversupply or falling memory prices.
   - *Where and when to check:* Memory makers' results; news in every daily report.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
3. **Revenue drops sharply in a chip-industry downturn, which would show sales are less repeatable than thought.** *(Thesis 2)*
