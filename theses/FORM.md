## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Before chips are cut from a wafer, each one is tested by touching it with thousands of tiny needles on a "probe card", and FormFactor makes those cards. Each card is custom-made for one chip design and wears out, so chipmakers keep buying new ones, especially for the high-bandwidth memory (HBM) used in AI. Revenue was a record $258.2 million last quarter (+31.9%), and one customer, SK hynix, was 24.3% of it [FormFactor Q2 2026 results, 2026-07-29; research file, 10-Q].

## How it makes money
- Probe cards (custom test cards) for memory, including high-bandwidth memory, and for GPU and custom AI chips; buyers are memory makers and chip designers (research file).
- The split by product and segment is not in our files; the deep dive will add it.

### Thesis 1: "AI chips and AI memory need more, and more complex, testing, so FormFactor sells more probe cards."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Record revenue (+31.9%), driven by high-bandwidth memory [FormFactor Q2 2026 results]; memory maker Micron's sales beat expectations [Micron 8-K, 2026-09-30].
2. **What must stay true?** FormFactor meets its guidance, and memory makers keep spending on AI memory.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Guidance is cut.
   - Memory makers (especially SK hynix, 24.3% of revenue) cut spending.

### Thesis 2: "Probe cards wear out and are custom-made for each chip design, so sales repeat."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** A new chip design needs a new card, and cards wear out, so customers keep buying (research file, product description; the deep dive will confirm it from the 10-K).
2. **What must stay true?** Revenue holds up reasonably well even when chipmakers build fewer factories.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Revenue drops sharply in a chip-industry downturn, which would show sales are less repeatable than thought.

### Thesis 3: "Becoming a second approved supplier for NVIDIA's chips could open a large new customer."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Deutsche Bank says FormFactor is now a second approved supplier of test cards for NVIDIA graphics chips [Investing.com, 2026-09-30]. Tier-3 report; not confirmed by the company.
2. **What must stay true?** The company confirms NVIDIA-related revenue in its results or filings.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - No confirmation in the next results or 10-Q.
   - Reports that NVIDIA chose other suppliers.
