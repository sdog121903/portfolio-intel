## What the company does (plain English)
NVIDIA designs the chips (GPUs) that most AI models are trained and run on, plus the complete
server systems, the networking that links thousands of chips together, and the software
(CUDA) developers use to program them. Its biggest customers are large cloud companies.

## How it makes money
Mostly its data-center business (chips, systems, networking); also gaming, professional
visualization and automotive. The deep dive will add the split, with sources.

## Metrics that matter
