## What the company does (plain English)
Bloom Energy makes fuel-cell power systems: boxes that turn natural gas or hydrogen into
electricity on site through a chemical reaction rather than burning, so a customer can make its
own power instead of waiting for the grid. Data centers, factories and utilities buy them.

## How it makes money
Selling and installing power systems, plus long-term service contracts to run and maintain them.
