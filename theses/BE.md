## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Bloom Energy makes fuel cells: refrigerator-sized boxes that turn natural gas into electricity on site through a chemical reaction instead of burning it. Data centers buy them because the power grid cannot connect new sites fast enough, so they make their own power. Revenue was $1,065.4 million last quarter, up 165.5% [Bloom Q2 2026 results, 2026-07-28].

## How it makes money
Selling and installing power systems, plus long-term service contracts to run and maintain them.

### Thesis 1: "AI data centers cannot wait years for the power grid, so on-site power from Bloom's fuel cells is in high demand."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Revenue grew 165.5% and the 2026 outlook was raised to $3.9-4.2bn [Bloom Q2 2026 results, 2026-07-28]; utilities are buying too (Ameren plans 500 megawatts, per Barclays' note, tier 3).
2. **What must stay true?** Bloom meets its 2026 outlook and keeps announcing new data-center and utility orders.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - The 2026 outlook is cut.
   - Large customers choose other on-site power (such as gas turbines or batteries) instead.

### Thesis 2: "Bloom has moved from burning cash to bringing cash in."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Operating cash flow (cash brought in by the day-to-day business) was $226.4m last quarter [Bloom Q2 2026 results].
2. **What must stay true?** Operating cash flow stays positive.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Operating cash flow turns negative again for two quarters.
   - A large share sale to raise money.

### Thesis 3: "Its biggest project, Oracle's Project Jupiter, goes ahead, just later than planned."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Oracle, Blue Owl and Bloom all say the contract remains in force despite the force-majeure notice [CNBC, 2026-09-24].
2. **What must stay true?** The gas pipeline arrives around February 2027 and installation starts.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - The Jupiter contract is cancelled or cut.
   - The pipeline slips well beyond 2027.

**The other side (reasons for caution):** Its biggest project is delayed, and a shareholder lawsuit is pending. The price is about 108 times expected profit, and the average analyst target is below Friday's close (MarketBeat, tier 3, unverified). Very jumpy: a typical day moves it 7.2% and its worst fall in a year was 52.7% (data/metrics).
