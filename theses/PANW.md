## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Palo Alto Networks sells cybersecurity: protection for company networks, cloud systems and logins, more and more as subscriptions that customers renew every year. It wants to be a "platform", meaning customers buy many security products from one company instead of from many small ones. Its key number, next-generation security ARR (annual recurring revenue: the yearly value of its newer subscriptions), reached $9.10 billion, up 63%, partly because it bought CyberArk [PANW Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-09-01].

## How it makes money
Product sales (firewall hardware) plus subscriptions and support, which are the growing part.

### Thesis 1: "Companies want fewer security suppliers, and Palo Alto is becoming the one-stop platform they consolidate on."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Next-generation security ARR grew 63% to $9.10bn and quarterly revenue 34% to $3.41bn, above forecasts [PANW Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-09-01].
2. **What must stay true?** Next-generation security ARR keeps growing after the one-time CyberArk boost fades, in line with the company's own forecast.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - The yearly revenue forecast ($14.10-14.20bn) is cut.
   - ARR growth falls well below the company's own plan for two quarters in a row.

### Thesis 2: "AI creates new things to protect (AI agents and models), and Palo Alto is building products for that."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** It launched AI-security services such as Unit 42 Frontier AI Defense (2026-09-22), and security stocks jumped about 13% on 2026-09-14 when AI-risk warnings made investors expect more security spending [Forbes, 2026-09-14].
2. **What must stay true?** AI-security products start to show up in the ARR and revenue the company reports.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** After several quarters the company still reports no traction for its AI-security products, or customers choose rivals for AI security.

### Thesis 3: "Adding CyberArk (logins and passwords for people and AI agents) makes the platform harder to replace."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** The $21.1bn purchase closed on 2026-02-11 and added identity security, the part that decides who and what may log in [Palo Alto Networks, 2026-02-11; PANW 10-Q, 2026-06-02].
2. **What must stay true?** The combined company meets its forecast, and the CyberArk products keep growing inside it.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - A write-down of the CyberArk purchase (admitting it was worth less than paid).
   - Guidance cut blamed on integration problems.

**The other side (reasons for caution):** The price is high: about 96-97 times next year's expected adjusted profit per share (report's calculation from company guidance). Bernstein downgraded it on 2026-09-18. Growth is guided to slow from 34% to 23-24%, it lost money under standard accounting last quarter, and the CyberArk deal added 112 million new shares.
