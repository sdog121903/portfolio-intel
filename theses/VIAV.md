## Why I own it (approved by Santi, 2026-10-05)

**In one sentence:** I hold VIAVI because every fast connection an AI data center builds has to be tested, and VIAVI sells the test tools.

Building faster networks inside and between data centers means testing every fibre link and connection at full speed, and VIAVI makes the instruments that do it, a bit like selling the diagnostic computers to the garages rather than betting on one car maker. That demand is visible: its network-test business (NSE) grew 69.2% to $353.9m last quarter, about half of the growth from data centers, and total revenue rose 52.5% to $443.1m, above its own guidance [VIAVI Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-05; research file]. Management aims for about $500m a quarter during 2027, which would make it a much bigger company than a year ago.

**What I accept (the risks):** three insiders sold about $4.2m in August-September outside pre-planned plans (which would have met my own insider-selling breaker at the time), a May share sale added about 15% more shares, and the recent 13-day winning streak rests more on momentum than on new facts.

## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
VIAVI sells test equipment: instruments that check whether networks, fibre links and data-center connections work at full speed, like a mechanic's diagnostic computer for internet cables. Companies buy them when they build or upgrade networks, and AI data centers are now a big source of that demand. Revenue was $443.1 million last quarter, up 52.5%, and its network-test business grew 69%, about half of it from data centers [VIAVI Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-05; research file].

## How it makes money
- Network and Service Enablement (NSE: network test equipment and software): $353.9m last quarter, up 69.2%, about half of the growth from data centers (Q4 FY2026 results, per research file).
- Optical Security and Performance Products (OSP: optical products; the deep dive will explain what they are): $89.2m, up 9.6% (same source).
- Quarterly revenue $443.1m (+52.5%); next quarter guided to $450-460m [VIAVI Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-05].

## Invalidation triggers (what would prove the thesis wrong)

   - *Why it matters:* It would mean data-center and network spending is slowing.
   - *Counts if:* A quarter below its guided range (Q1 FY2027: $450m) or next-quarter guidance below the current quarter.
   - *Early warning:* Guidance only flat with the current quarter.
   - *Where and when to check:* Quarterly results; next about 2026-10-28.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
2. **Management says data-center testing demand has slowed.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* Data centers are the growth engine; a slowdown there removes the main reason.
   - *Counts if:* Management says data-center demand is slowing, or NSE growth falls below 20%.
   - *Early warning:* NSE growth falls by more than half from one quarter to the next.
   - *Where and when to check:* Earnings calls.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
3. **The $500m aim is withdrawn or pushed out.** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* It is management's own yardstick for the bigger-company case.
   - *Counts if:* Management drops the aim or moves it beyond 2027.
   - *Early warning:* Management stops repeating it on calls.
