## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
VIAVI sells test equipment: instruments that check whether networks, fibre links and data-center connections work at full speed, like a mechanic's diagnostic computer for internet cables. Companies buy them when they build or upgrade networks, and AI data centers are now a big source of that demand. Revenue was $443.1 million last quarter, up 52.5%, and its network-test business grew 69%, about half of it from data centers [VIAVI Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-05; research file].

## How it makes money
- Network and Service Enablement (NSE: network test equipment and software): $353.9m last quarter, up 69.2%, about half of the growth from data centers (Q4 FY2026 results, per research file).
- Optical Security and Performance Products (OSP: optical products; the deep dive will explain what they are): $89.2m, up 9.6% (same source).
- Quarterly revenue $443.1m (+52.5%); next quarter guided to $450-460m [VIAVI Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-05].
