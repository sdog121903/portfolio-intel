## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Sterling is a US construction company that prepares the ground for very large buildings: it clears and levels land, pours foundations and concrete, and lays utility lines. Most of its growth now comes from data centers and big factories, which need huge, well-prepared sites fast. It is paid per project, so its backlog (signed work not yet done), $4.33 billion and up 116%, shows how much revenue is already lined up [Sterling Q2 2026 results, 2026-08-03].

### Thesis 1: "Sterling is one of the first builders every new data center needs, so it gets paid early in the AI building boom."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Revenue grew 90% to $1.168bn and backlog more than doubled to $4.33bn; data centers are most of the growth (the last point is inference, medium confidence) [Sterling Q2 2026 results, 2026-08-03].
2. **What must stay true?** Backlog keeps growing, and the 2026 outlook ($4.00-4.15bn) is met.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Backlog shrinks for two quarters in a row.
   - The 2026 outlook is cut.
   - Big tech companies announce cuts to data-center building plans.

### Thesis 2: "Signed work already in hand makes Sterling's next year of revenue easier to see than for most companies."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Backlog of $4.33bn is about one year of revenue at the 2026 outlook of $4.00-4.15bn (our calculation) [Sterling Q2 2026 results].
2. **What must stay true?** New orders keep at least replacing the work it completes (book-to-bill, new orders divided by revenue, at 1 or above).
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Customers cancel or delay large projects.
   - Book-to-bill stays below 1 for several quarters.

**The other side (reasons for caution):** It moves about three times as much as the market (beta 3.29) and fell 54% from its June high at its worst (data/metrics). Data centers are most of the growth, so an AI building slowdown would hit it directly. The August results showed revenue rising faster than profit margins, and the shelf filing allows share sales at any time.
