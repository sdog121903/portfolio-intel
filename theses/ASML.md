## Why I own it (approved by Santi, 2026-10-05)

**In one sentence:** I hold ASML because every advanced chip in the world, including every leading AI chip, is printed on a machine only ASML can build.

ASML is the only company that makes EUV (extreme ultraviolet) lithography machines, the giant printers that draw the smallest circuit patterns onto silicon. Every chipmaker making the newest chips (TSMC, Samsung, Intel and the memory makers) has to buy from it, so ASML sits at a toll gate on the whole AI chip industry rather than betting on one chip design winning. AI spending is now pushing those chipmakers to build more factories: ASML raised its 2026 sales forecast twice this year, to EUR 43-45bn from EUR 36-40bn, and says its 2027 machine capacity is almost fully booked [ASML 6-K, 2026-07-15]. On top of new machines, every machine already sold keeps earning service and upgrade money for years (EUR 8.2bn, 25% of 2025 sales) [20-F FY2025], which softens the bad years that chip equipment always has.

**What I accept (the risks):** the price already expects a lot (about 60 times the last 12 months of profit), demand moves in cycles (2024 sales grew only 2.6%), and China (29.1% of 2025 sales) can be restricted by governments at any time. My triggers below watch exactly those risks.

### Thesis 1: "ASML has a monopoly on the machines needed to make the most advanced chips, so it can keep charging high prices."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** No competitor makes EUV machines. ASML shipped 48 in 2025 (44 in 2024), and its gross margin (the share of each sale left after the cost of making the product) rose from 51.3% (2024) to 52.8% (2025) and 54.0% (Q2 2026), a sign it can raise prices [20-F FY2025; 6-K 2026-07-15].
2. **What must stay true?** ASML stays the only EUV supplier, and its gross margin stays above 50%. *(pillars 1 and 2 below)*
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these *(triggers 1, 2 and 3 below)*:
   - A competitor shows a working EUV machine.
   - Gross margin falls below 50% in a quarter.
   - Its single optics supplier, Carl Zeiss, has a serious problem.

### Thesis 2: "AI is pushing chipmakers to build more factories, so ASML's sales keep growing fast in 2026-2027."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** ASML raised its 2026 forecast twice. EUR 43-45bn would be about 32-38% more than 2025's EUR 32.7bn; it says 2027 is almost fully booked and it is adding about 30% more capacity [6-K 2026-07-15].
2. **What must stay true?** Chipmakers keep spending on new factories, and ASML hits its forecast quarter by quarter, starting with Q3 on 2026-10-14. *(pillars 3 and 4 below)*
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these *(triggers 4, 5 and 6 below)*:
   - The 2026 sales forecast is cut below EUR 43bn.
   - TSMC or Samsung announce cuts to factory spending.
   - The MATCH Act becomes law and cuts off China (about 20% of 2026 sales, per management).

### Thesis 3: "Every machine ASML sells keeps paying for years through service and upgrades, which cushions bad years."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Service and upgrades brought in EUR 8.2bn in 2025, 25% of sales and up 26%; management expects over 30% growth in 2026 [20-F FY2025; Q2 call per Investing.com, tier 3].
2. **What must stay true?** Service revenue keeps growing, including in years when new-machine sales slow. *(pillar 5 below)*
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these *(triggers 7 and 8 below)*:

## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
ASML, a Dutch company, builds giant machines that use light to "print" tiny circuit patterns onto silicon wafers, the thin discs that become computer chips. It is the only company in the world that makes the most advanced kind, called EUV (extreme ultraviolet), so every maker of the newest chips (TSMC, Samsung, Intel and the memory makers) must buy from it. It earns money twice: once when it sells a machine, and again for years by servicing and upgrading it (25% of 2025 sales) [20-F FY2025].

## How it makes money
- New systems: €24,474.3m, 74.9% of 2025 sales (EUV and DUV lithography, metrology) [20-F FY2025, Item 5]
- Service and upgrades of installed machines ("installed base management"): €8,193.0m, 25.1%, +26.2% [20-F FY2025, Item 5]
- Customers pay large deposits ahead of delivery (contract liabilities €19,372.6m at end-2025) [20-F FY2025 balance sheet]

## Metrics that matter
- Quarterly sales vs guidance (2026 guided €43–45bn) [6-K 2026-07-15]
- Gross margin vs guidance (2025: 52.8%; Q2 2026: 54.0%; Q3 guide 55–57%)
- EUV systems sold and 2027 capacity plan (+~30% low-NA EUV)
- Service (installed base) sales growth
- China share of sales and export-control changes (China 29.1% of 2025 sales; about 20% expected in 2026)

## What must stay true (checkable pillars)
Each pillar says why it matters, how it is measured, where it stands now and when it is next checked. The daily report tests every one; when a "Now" line changes, the change log records it.

1. **ASML stays the only EUV supplier.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* The monopoly is what lets ASML set prices and what makes every advanced chipmaker dependent on it.
   - *Measured by:* No other company delivers an EUV machine to a chipmaker for production use (tier-1 or tier-2 source).
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* Sole supplier; 48 EUV systems recognised in 2025, up from 44 in 2024 [20-F FY2025].
   - *Next check:* Every daily report (news); Q3 results on 2026-10-14.
2. **Gross margin stays above 50%.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* A high margin is the proof of pricing power: customers pay up because they have no alternative.
   - *Measured by:* Quarterly gross margin in each results release (6-K).
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* 54.0% in Q2 2026; 55-57% guided for Q3; 52.8% for 2025 [6-K 2026-07-15; 20-F FY2025].
   - *Next check:* Q3 results, 2026-10-14.
3. **Sales track the 2026 forecast of EUR 43-45bn.** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* The forecast is the company's own promise; hitting it shows the AI factory build-out is real, not just talk.
   - *Measured by:* Each quarter's sales against the company's guided range, and whether the full-year range is kept, raised or cut.
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* Q2 sales EUR 9.3bn beat expectations; Q3 guided to EUR 11.0-12.0bn; full year EUR 43-45bn [6-K 2026-07-15].
   - *Next check:* Q3 results, 2026-10-14.
4. **Chipmakers keep expanding advanced capacity.** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* ASML's customers' building plans are ASML's future orders; four customers were 61.2% of 2025 sales [20-F FY2025].
   - *Measured by:* Capital-spending plans in TSMC's and Samsung's results, and ASML's comments on 2027 demand.
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* ASML says 2027 capacity is nearly fully booked and it is adding about 30% more [6-K 2026-07-15].
   - *Next check:* TSMC Q3 results 2026-10-15; ASML Q4 results (January 2027).
5. **Service and upgrade revenue keeps growing.** *(Thesis 3)*
   - *Why it matters:* It is the steady part of the business: it keeps coming in when new-machine sales slow, which cushions the cycle.
   - *Measured by:* Installed-base management revenue against a year earlier, in each results release.
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* EUR 8,193.0m in 2025 (+26.2%); management expects over 30% growth in 2026 [20-F FY2025; Q2 call per Investing.com, tier 3].
   - *Next check:* Q3 results, 2026-10-14.
6. **China shrinks gradually, not suddenly.** *(whole position; added 2026-10-05)*
   - *Why it matters:* China was the biggest region in 2025; a gradual fall is planned for, a sudden cut-off is not.
   - *Measured by:* China's share of sales in each quarter, and any export rule that changes what ASML may ship or service.
   - *Now (as of 2026-10-04):* 29.1% of 2025 sales; about 20% expected for 2026 (management) [20-F FY2025; Reuters, 2026-07-15].
   - *Next check:* Q3 results 2026-10-14; US defence-bill (NDAA) timetable, late 2026.

## Invalidation triggers (what would prove the thesis wrong)
Each trigger says why it matters, exactly what counts, the early warning that usually comes first, where to look and its status now. A trigger that fires becomes a THESIS ALERT in the daily report: a reason to re-read this file, not an instruction to sell. An early warning is reported as "Watch".

1. **A competitor shows a working EUV machine.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* It would end the monopoly that justifies ASML's high margins and its high price.
   - *Counts if:* A company other than ASML delivers an EUV machine to a chipmaker for production, confirmed by a tier-1 or tier-2 source.
   - *Early warning:* A credible report of a rival EUV prototype printing chips in a lab (for example in China).
   - *Where and when to check:* News in every daily report; ASML's 20-F competition section (February each year).
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
2. **Gross margin falls below 50% in a quarter.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* Margin below 50% would say customers no longer pay up, which is the core of the monopoly case.
   - *Counts if:* A reported quarterly gross margin under 50.0%.
   - *Early warning:* Guidance for the next quarter below 52%, or a quarter that lands below its own guided range.
   - *Where and when to check:* Every quarterly results release; next 2026-10-14.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
3. **Its single optics supplier, Carl Zeiss, has a serious problem.** *(Thesis 1)*
   - *Why it matters:* Zeiss makes all of ASML's EUV optics; ASML cannot ship machines without them [20-F FY2025].
   - *Counts if:* ASML says a Zeiss problem is delaying shipments or cutting its forecast.
   - *Early warning:* Reports of a fire, strike, quality problem or capacity limit at Carl Zeiss SMT.
   - *Where and when to check:* ASML 6-K filings and news in every daily report.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
4. **The 2026 sales forecast is cut below EUR 43bn.** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* It would mean the AI factory build-out is slower than the company itself expected three months ago.
   - *Counts if:* Full-year 2026 sales guidance below EUR 43bn.
   - *Early warning:* Q3 sales below the EUR 11.0bn bottom of the guided range, or the full-year range narrowed to its low end.
   - *Where and when to check:* Q3 results, 2026-10-14.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
5. **TSMC or Samsung announce cuts to factory spending.** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* They are ASML's biggest customers; their spending plans are ASML's order book.
   - *Counts if:* Either company lowers its capital-spending plan for the year below its previous plan.
   - *Early warning:* Talk of delaying new factories or of a pause to absorb capacity already bought.
   - *Where and when to check:* TSMC Q3 results 2026-10-15; Samsung's Q3 results (late October).
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
6. **The MATCH Act becomes law and cuts off China (about 20% of 2026 sales, per management).** *(Thesis 2)*
   - *Why it matters:* It would remove a fifth of planned sales and part of the service income at once.
   - *Counts if:* A US law or Dutch rule takes effect that stops ASML's DUV immersion sales to major Chinese chipmakers, or their servicing.
   - *Early warning:* The bill passes one chamber of Congress or is included in the final defence-bill (NDAA) text.
   - *Where and when to check:* US Congress record; ASML 6-K filings; Dutch government statements.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit. Early warning partly present: reported as heading into the defence bill (tier-3 sources only, unconfirmed).
7. **Service revenue shrinks.** *(Thesis 3)*
   - *Why it matters:* Service is the cushion in bad years; if it shrinks, ASML is fully exposed to the equipment cycle.
   - *Counts if:* Installed-base management revenue lower than a year earlier for two quarters in a row.
   - *Early warning:* One quarter lower than a year earlier.
   - *Where and when to check:* Every quarterly results release; next 2026-10-14.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
8. **A rule bans servicing machines already installed in China.** *(Thesis 3)*
   - *Why it matters:* It would cut part of the steady service income, not just future sales.
   - *Counts if:* A law or rule in force that bans servicing installed ASML machines in China.
   - *Early warning:* Draft legislation that includes servicing (reported for the MATCH Act, tier 3).
   - *Where and when to check:* Same as the MATCH Act trigger.
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit. Early warning present: reported draft text includes servicing (tier 3, unconfirmed).
9. **Management says 2027 sales will not grow.** *(whole position; added 2026-10-05)*
   - *Why it matters:* Today's price assumes years of growth; a flat 2027 would mean the cycle has turned.
   - *Counts if:* ASML's first 2027 outlook (usually given with Q4 results) is flat or down from 2026.
   - *Early warning:* Management stops describing 2027 as a growth year, or says capacity is no longer fully booked.
   - *Where and when to check:* Q3 results 2026-10-14 (comments) and Q4 results, January 2027 (outlook).
   - *Status now (2026-10-04):* Not hit.
