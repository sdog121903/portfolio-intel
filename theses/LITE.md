## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Inside AI data centers, thousands of chips must exchange data, and over longer distances that is done with laser light travelling through glass fibre. Lumentum makes the lasers and optical parts that turn electrical signals into light, plus switches that steer light between fibres. Its buyers are module makers and cloud giants (two customers were 41.6% of last year's revenue), and last quarter its revenue more than doubled to $1.01 billion [Lumentum Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-11].

## How it makes money
- Lasers and optical components, mainly for cloud and AI data centers (other markets and the split: the deep dive will add them).
- Optical modules and optical circuit switches that steer light (unit sales not disclosed).
- Two customers were 41.6% of last fiscal year's revenue (10-K, per research file).

### Thesis 1: "AI data centers need ever more laser links, and Lumentum makes the key lasers, with demand running ahead of what it can ship."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Revenue grew 109% to $1.01bn; next quarter is guided to $1.225-1.275bn; management says it is still shipping behind demand [Lumentum Q4 FY2026 results, 2026-08-11; call transcript, tier 3]. It locked in wafer supply with a six-year AXT deal [AXT 8-K, 2026-07-29].
2. **What must stay true?** Lumentum meets its revenue guidance, and demand for its lasers stays bigger than its supply.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Revenue guidance is cut.
   - Management says supply has caught up with demand and prices are falling.
   - One of its two biggest customers cuts orders.

### Thesis 2: "US moves to keep Chinese optical parts out of sensitive networks could shift orders to US makers such as Lumentum."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** A Senate bill (2026-09-15) and draft rules aim to keep Chinese transceivers out of US national-security systems [Sen. McCormick, 2026-09-15]. Speculative: none of it is law yet.
2. **What must stay true?** The bill or rules move forward in Congress or at the agencies.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - The bill dies or is watered down.
   - Lumentum reports no change in orders from US customers.

**The other side (reasons for caution):** Very jumpy: it fell 9.9% on 2026-09-14 and lost 42.8% at its worst point in the past year (data/metrics). Two customers are 41.6% of revenue. The bond swap added about 13% more shares, and the price is about 31 times yearly sales (tier-3 estimate, unverified).
