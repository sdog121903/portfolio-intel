## What the company does (plain English)
Snowflake runs a cloud data platform: companies store their data with it and analyse it (and
increasingly build AI tools on it) without running their own servers. Customers mostly pay for
how much computing they use, not a fixed seat price.

## How it makes money
Mostly usage-based product revenue (customers pay for the computing they consume), plus some
services. The deep dive will add the split, with sources.

## Metrics that matter
- Product revenue growth
- Net revenue retention (do existing customers spend more each year?)
- Remaining performance obligation (contracted future revenue)
- Free cash flow margin and stock-based compensation
- Guidance for product revenue

## What must stay true (proposed starting points: edit or delete)
1. Existing customers keep using more, so usage revenue keeps growing.
2. AI and data workloads add to demand rather than moving to rivals.

## Invalidation triggers (proposed starting points: edit or delete)
1. Net revenue retention keeps falling for several quarters without a stated cause.
2. Large customers move their data work to a cheaper or rival platform.
