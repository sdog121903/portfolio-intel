## What the company does (plain English, 3 sentences)
Snowflake stores companies' data in the cloud and lets them analyse it, for example to see sales by region or to feed AI tools. Customers pay for the computing they use, like a taxi meter, so the more work they do on their data (including AI work), the more Snowflake bills. Product revenue was $1,491.9 million last quarter, up 37%, and existing customers spent 26% more than a year earlier (net revenue retention of 126%) [Snowflake Q2 FY2027 results, 2026-09-02].

## How it makes money
Mostly usage-based product revenue (customers pay for the computing they consume), plus some
services. The deep dive will add the split, with sources.

### Thesis 1: "AI makes companies do much more work on their data, and Snowflake bills by usage, so AI raises its revenue."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Growth sped up for three quarters in a row to 37%; the company says its new AI tools drove about half of the speed-up [Snowflake Q2 FY2027 results, 2026-09-02; research file].
2. **What must stay true?** Product revenue growth stays near its recent pace, and next quarter's guidance ($1,588-1,593m) is met.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Product revenue guidance is cut.
   - Growth slows sharply for two quarters in a row.

### Thesis 2: "Customers who join keep spending more every year."

1. **Why would I own it for this?** Net revenue retention was 126%: the same customers spent 26% more than a year earlier [Snowflake Q2 FY2027 results].
2. **What must stay true?** Net revenue retention stays well above 100%.
3. **What would prove me wrong?** Any one of these:
   - Net revenue retention falls toward 100% (existing customers stop growing their spending).
   - Contracted future revenue keeps shrinking quarter after quarter.

**The other side (reasons for caution):** Contracted future revenue slipped from $9.21bn to $9.00bn in the quarter, and it still loses money under standard accounting ($191.7m last quarter). The new bonds could add 2.2-4% more shares. A securities class action is pending. The stock is up about 188% from its April low, so expectations are high.
