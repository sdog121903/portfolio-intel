## What the company does (plain English)
AMD designs processors: CPUs (the general-purpose brain of a computer) for PCs and servers, and
GPUs (chips that do many calculations at once) for gaming and AI data centers. It does not make
chips itself; outside factories, mainly TSMC, manufacture them.

## How it makes money
Chip sales grouped into segments such as data center, PCs, gaming and embedded chips. The deep
dive will add each segment's share of revenue, with sources.
